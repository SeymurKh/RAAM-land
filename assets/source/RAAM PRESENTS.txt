GRAPE GROOVES
Grape Grooves is a musical journey that blends
the artistry of our residents, the charm of
Azerbaijan’s nature, and the beauty of
winemaking culture.
We craft atmospheric video sets in stunning
locations - amidst vineyards, wineries, and their
surroundings.
This project captures the harmony of music and
nature, revealing the spirit of each region through
sounds, visuals, and emotions.

-- 6 of 23 --

RAAM × FOMO
RAAM x FOMO is a collaborative project created to organize
special events featuring residents of the RAAM label at one of
the most well-known clubs in Azerbaijan - FOMO Baku.
This collaboration provides a platform for our resident artists
to showcase their talent, energy, and ability to connect with a
live audience in a major club environment.


As the first step of this collaboration, we produced a series of
short interviews with participating artists as well as with the
booking manager of the venue.


In addition, we filmed two full video sets, which will soon be
released on our official YouTube channel.

Further collaborative events and formats are currently in
development.

UpTempo Jams
Uptempo Jams is an audio-visual project built around
predominantly high-BPM electronic music sets, created to
capture the energy and dynamics of fast-paced electronic
music.


The sets are recorded at Finestra del Aperitivo, providing a
unique and recognizable atmosphere for the project.


The series features not only RAAM residents, but also artists
from our wider community - including DJs who regularly
perform at our venues, as well as emerging artists who have
completed our coaching programs and are ready to present
their sound in a recorded set format.


Through this project, we aim to highlight both established and
rising talents within our ecosystem while building a growing
archive of high-energy electronic music performances.

RAAM Cassette
RAAM Cassette is an ode to memories and the atmosphere
born from the live sound of electronic music. We preserve
moments, creating a bridge between the past and the present.
Just as cassettes stored the music of childhood, RAAM
Cassette captures live sets by artists performed in spaces
where music is the soul of the venue.
These recordings allow listeners to relive unique moments by
replaying them in the same locations. This is not just
background music - it’s living memories and the energy of the
moment, highlighting the uniqueness of places associated with
RAAM Label.

-- 9 of 23 --

RAAM Live
RAAM Live is a series of recorded audio sets captured directly
during live DJ performances in our venues.


The concept is simple: DJs performing on a given night record the
audio version of the set they play live. After some time, these
recordings are released as part of the RAAM Live series.


The “Live” aspect highlights the authenticity of the format - each
set is recorded in real time during the performance, preserving the
natural flow, energy, and atmosphere of the night.


Through this series, we aim to document and share the sound of
our events while building a growing archive of live performances
from both resident and guest DJs within our ecosystem.

Coaching (DJ'ing)
Our DJ coaching program offers both group and individual
sessions, designed to help artists build the technical and
creative foundation of DJing.
The program concludes with a graduation performance at
Biblioteka Baku, where students perform in a real club
environment.

After completing the program, DJs can begin their professional
activity. The most promising artists may also join our
community and gain access to new opportunities within our
ecosystem.

-- 11 of 23 --

Coaching

(Music Production)
The goal of the production program is for each participant to
create an original track by the end of the course.
At the final stage, artists present their work for review.
Depending on the artist, their ambitions, and the quality of the
track, it may be performed in our venues or supported by the
label through promotion and production opportunities.
