PEDRO [PT]
Hailing from Portugal, Pedro is a passionate selector, seamlessly
moving between deep house, funky house, minimal, and tech house.
His journey began in the 90s as a light jockey in a club, where he was
captivated by the DJs behind the decks - a spark that ignited a
lifelong dedication to music.


With over 20 years on stage, Pedro has performed at iconic European
venues, shaping dancefloors and defining himself as a dedicated
music lover and digger, always searching for the perfect track to
elevate the moment. His sets are carefully crafted journeys, blending
groove, rhythm, and emotion to create unforgettable atmospheres.


Whether performing in intimate venues or on larger stages, Pedro
brings authenticity, passion, and unwavering devotion to the craft,
ensuring every performance resonates deeply with the crowd.
House • Deep House • Tech House • Indie Dance

BORAA [CA]
One of the key figures behind the modern DJ scene in Azerbaijan,
contributing to the sound and development of some of the country’s
leading venues.


With nearly two decades in the electronic music scene, he has
performed in Istanbul, Los Angeles, and across several cities in
Canada.


Throughout his career, he has shared the stage with artists such as
Guy J, Jody Wisternoff, Frankey & Sandrino, Martin Roth, Kasper
Koman, Alex Orion, Tantum, Musumeci, and Simply City.


Alongside his DJ career, Boraa is also a music producer, creating his
own material in the progressive and organic house spectrum. His
productions have helped establish his artistic identity and have
allowed him to make a notable mark within the scene.
Deep Progressive • Deep Hypnotic •

Melodic House • Organic House
